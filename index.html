Three feelings under your fingertips.

Every switch is really just a tiny decision about how a keystroke should feel — smooth, bumpy, or loud.

L
Linear
Straight down, no resistance.
A smooth, uninterrupted press from top to bottom — favored for speed and for gaming, where every millisecond of travel counts.
T
Tactile
A small bump tells you it registered.
A physical bump partway through the keystroke gives quiet feedback without a click — the office-friendly middle ground.
C
Clicky
Loud on purpose.
A built-in click leaf turns typing into a percussion instrument. Beloved by enthusiasts, banned from most open offices.

Why people who type for a living fall down this rabbit hole.

It starts with one board that feels slightly better than the last. Then a different switch. Then keycaps that match nothing else in the room. It's a small, satisfying loop — tune the input device you touch thousands of times a day, and the work itself feels a little lighter.
